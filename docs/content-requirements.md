# Institutional Content Verification & Placeholder Checklist

This document tracks all institutional details that require formal review and official input from the Sanskrit Vedic Gurukul and Vedic Gurukul Trust prior to production release.

---

## 1. Gurukul Institutional Data

- [ ] **Official Institutional Legal Name**: Sanskrit and English registered titles.
- [ ] **Physical Campus Address**: Full postal address, pin code, district, and nearest transport links.
- [ ] **Official Telephone Numbers**: Admissions helpline and reception contact.
- [ ] **Official Email Addresses**: `admissions@`, `trust@`, `contact@`.
- [ ] **Founding Year & Milestones**: Verified historical dates and milestone timeline.
- [ ] **Faculty & Acharyas Directory**: Official names, academic titles, photos, and qualifications of the Kulapati and teaching Acharyas.
- [ ] **Accreditation / University Affiliation**: Details of state Sanskrit university or Vedic examination board certifications.

---

## 2. Vedic Gurukul Trust Data

- [ ] **Trust Legal Registration Number**: Verified registration under the Indian Trusts Act.
- [ ] **80G & 12A Certificates**: Income Tax exemption registration numbers and validity periods.
- [ ] **Trust PAN Number**: Official PAN card details for donation receipts.
- [ ] **Official Bank Account Details**: Bank name, branch, account number, and IFSC code for wire transfers.
- [ ] **FCRA Status (if applicable)**: Clearance details for receiving foreign contributions.
- [ ] **Auditor Information**: Name and firm of the Statutory Chartered Accountants.

---

## 3. Visual & Media Assets

- [ ] **High-Resolution Photography**: Actual photographs of campus grounds, classrooms, Yajnashala, library, and students.
- [ ] **Official Logo & Emblem**: High-resolution vector SVG of the official institutional seal.
- [ ] **Syllabus Documents**: Downloadable official PDF files of detailed curriculum outlines.
